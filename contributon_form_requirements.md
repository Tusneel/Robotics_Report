An individual reflective report must be submitted separately via the appropriate MyAberdeen submission area.
The file should be named reflective report[SURNAME].pdf, where SURNAME is your surname. The reflective
report should contain an account of your contributions in the group, the perceived contributions of your group
members, what you learned during the project, and what the main challenges were that you faced (individually).
This has a 500-word limit (include a word counter at the end)
